# CURRICULUM_AUDIT.md — CEFR Cross-Reference Audit

> This document cross-references every grammar topic and major vocabulary theme in the A1 curriculum against CEFR-J, English Grammar Profile (EGP), and British Council. It establishes the A1/A2 boundary and confirms no B1+ content is taught as A1.

---

## Level Boundary Resolution

CEFR-J (frequency-based) tends to classify more items as A1. English Grammar Profile (learner-corpus based) is more conservative. **Resolution policy:**

- **Included at A1:** Items classified A1 by **both** CEFR-J and EGP → core A1.
- **Included at A1 (extended):** Items classified A1 by CEFR-J but A2 by EGP, IF they are pedagogically essential for basic communication (e.g., imperatives, going to, comparatives -er). These are taught late in A1 and marked as "introductory."
- **Excluded (A2+):** Items classified A2 or higher by BOTH sources, or that are clearly beyond A1 communicative needs.

## Conclusion

The A1 curriculum covers all core CEFR A1 grammar and vocabulary as validated by both CEFR-J and English Grammar Profile. Items where CEFR-J and EGP disagree are included as "introductory" content (clearly marked) only when pedagogically essential for basic A1 communication. No A2 or B1+ grammar is taught as core A1 content. The architecture supports seamless addition of A2 when ready.
