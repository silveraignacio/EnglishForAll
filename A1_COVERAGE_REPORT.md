## Gaps & Notes

1. **Listening:** No audio content in MVP (by design). Architecture includes `audioUrl` on vocabulary and examples; a Listening component can be added to the data model and a dedicated Listening exercise type without restructuring.
2. **Speaking:** No speech-recognition in MVP. Preparation is done via reading-aloud-ready sentences, translation exercises, and dialogue-response selection. Architecture ready for a Speaking module.
3. **Writing production:** Full free-text writing exercises are limited to translation at MVP; the final exam includes a writing section that can be extended with free-form writing (the exercise model supports `acceptedAnswers` + approximate matching).
4. **these/those, more/most comparatives, should/must, present perfect:** Intentionally deferred to A2 (see CURRICULUM_AUDIT.md).
5. **Phrasal verbs:** Only basic daily-routine phrasals (get up, wake up) at A1; fuller phrasal verb coverage deferred to A2.
